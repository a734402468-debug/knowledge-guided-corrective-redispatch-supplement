# Representative Soft-Priority Cases

## Limit_Scen_086: lower-priority resources avoided

- Baseline 1: AC-valid in 4 iterations; Level 1 supplies 293.47 MW (100%) of the first-round commanded redispatch.
- Baseline 2: AC-valid in 4 iterations; Level 1+Level 2 = 0; Level 3 = 136.00 MW (36.415%); Level 4 = 237.47 MW (63.585%).
- Proposed method: AC-valid in 3 iterations; Level 1+Level 2 = 0; Level 3 = 93.79 MW (26.605%); Level 4 = 258.74 MW (73.395%).

This case illustrates avoidance of lower-priority directions when higher-priority flexibility is sufficient.

## Limit_Scen_019: lower-priority backup retained

- Baseline 1: AC-valid in 2 iterations; Level 1 = 257.33 MW (67.562%), Level 2 = 64.55 MW, and Level 4 = 59.00 MW.
- Baseline 2: does not obtain an AC-valid corrective dispatch within the adopted procedure.
- Proposed method: AC-valid in 1 iteration; Level 1 = 120.80 MW (31.716%) and Level 4 = 260.08 MW (68.284%).

This case illustrates that the soft formulation can retain a lower-priority direction as corrective backup. The comparison does not claim that low-priority availability is the only causal reason for the hard-baseline failure.
